Games Fair brings free and open source games to phones, tablets, desktops, and the web.

This journal is an example of content owned by the project. It is a Markdown file rendered by Astro, alongside the downloads and screenshot gallery supplied by daysite.

## Make the site your own

The App Fair header and footer are theme components. A project can keep them, replace them, or create entirely different pages with Astro content collections, MDX, or its preferred integrations.

The app's screenshots, supported platforms, and release downloads continue to come from the publication pipeline.
